About CampusMart

Our Purpose

CampusMart is a student marketplace designed to help university students buy and sell second-hand items.

What Can You Buy and Sell?

  • Textbooks and study materials
  • Calculators and electronics
  • Furniture
  • Clothing and stationery

How It Works

  1. Find an item you need.
  2. Contact the seller to arrange a purchase.
  3. Meet safely on campus to exchange the item.
